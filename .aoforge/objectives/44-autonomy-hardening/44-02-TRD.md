<anti_patterns>
- Replacing the read line with a different path, such as `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/agents/...`. Typed
  subagents get their definition as the system prompt, so no read is needed at all.
- Removing the read line from a spawn that still uses `general-purpose` without also switching it
  to the typed agent. That silently strips the role (research-objective's two spawns).
- Keeping a "Write SUMMARY.md" instruction anywhere in research-synthesizer.md.
- Having the orchestrator summarise or reword the synthesizer's text. Write it verbatim.
</anti_patterns>

  final message minus a leading `## SYNTHESIS COMPLETE` header block. Document that fallback in
  both orchestrators.
