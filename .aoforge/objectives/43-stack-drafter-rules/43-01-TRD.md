# TRD 43-01: Runner readers — Make variable expansion (D1) and internal Taskfile tasks (D5)

<objective>
Fix two runner-reader defects from objective 42's rollout. Both are small, local fixes in `stack-runners.cjs`, and both feed every later drafter TRD.

- **D1 (devflowops shape).** A root aggregate target `build: frontend backend` was classified `off_stack` and dropped, because the Go leg's recipe is `$(GO) build ...` and `parseMakefile` never expands variables. `$(GO)` is an unknown tool, so the leg never classifies, `scopeOf` sees only node, and the drafter falls back to a CI variant (`go build -o gitea_no_gcc`) or a single leg (`make lint-spell`). Research reproduced this: replacing `$(GO)` with `go` in the same fixture already gives `make build/test/lint`.
- **D5 (ao-terminal shape).** Taskfile tasks marked `internal: true` (`go:mod:tidy`, `npm:install`) can't be invoked from the CLI. Even so, they are proposed as candidates, and `stack verify` reports them `resolved`.

Purpose: aggregate targets win when they include the primary stack, and internal tasks are never proposed or reported as runnable.
Output: expansion in the parser, an `internal` flag through parse → hasTarget → candidates, and unit and e2e regressions.
</objective>

<anti_patterns>
- Evaluating make semantics (`$(shell ...)`, `$(wildcard ...)`, conditionals, target-specific vars, `+=`). Use only bounded literal substitution, and leave anything else verbatim.
- Expanding at classification time in stack-evidence. Expansion belongs in the parser, so `hasTarget`, bodies and dep expansion all share it.
- Dropping internal tasks from `parsed.tasks`. Bodies are still needed for dep/call expansion (`targetUnits`). Flag them; don't delete them.
- Treating `internal: "true"` (string) and `internal: true` the same as a templated value. Accept `true`/`"true"` only, and treat anything else as false.
</anti_patterns>
