# Built-in sweep inventory

Status: inventory (TRD 62-02). Conversions: TRDs 62-04 to 62-09 and 62-11. Closed: TRD 62-10.

This is the list of every prompt in the 34 skills and 40 active workflows that the built-in sweep converts or deliberately keeps, with the exact planned conversion, plus the planned progress tasks, plan-mode draft reviews and `allowed-tools` changes. The rules the conversions follow are in [references/built-ins.md](../plugins/devflow/devflow/references/built-ins.md), and the CI ratchet that checks this list against the prose is [builtin-sweep.repo.test.cjs](../plugins/devflow/devflow/bin/lib/builtin-sweep.repo.test.cjs).

## Prompts

120 rows (68 `scan`, 52 `manual`). Each group's rows are the work list of its conversion TRD.

Columns: `ID` is `BS-nnn` in table order (group order of `GROUPS`, then file, then line). `Detect` is `scan` when the scanner's choice patterns or schema checks flag the line, else `manual` (TRD 62-03 settles it). `Kind` is one of `choice` (a discrete choice written as prose), `free-text` (an open answer that stays prose), `explanatory` (a line that describes or displays a prompt), `subagent` (a prompt inside a flow a subagent runs), `ask-misuse` (AskUserQuestion used without options) or `schema` (AskUserQuestion that breaks the tool's limits or lacks a header). `Before` is a verbatim excerpt of one line, at least 12 characters, with `|` written `\|`.

Conversion forms: `AskUserQuestion header "<12 max>": <labels>` (the recommended option first with `(Recommended)`; routing, gating and the existing outcomes unchanged); `allow marker: <reason>` (a `builtin-audit: allow` comment on the line or the line above, never inside a fence Claude prints); `reword: <new text>`; `plain-text ask: "<question>"`; `checkpoint:decision return` (the subagent returns a checkpoint and the orchestrator asks); `keep: <reason>` (the scanner does not flag the line, so a marker would be stale). `(x2)` means two identical lines in the file, each handled as stated.

Bare `Options:` list heads cannot be quoted in `Before` (the 12-character minimum), so each is covered by the row named here, whose Conversion deletes it: workflows/execute-objective.md line 1154 (BS-061); workflows/security-audit.md line 59 (BS-112); workflows/transition.md line 102 (BS-073); workflows/transition.md line 519 (BS-074); workflows/workstreams-merge.md line 53 (BS-093). Line numbers are as of this inventory.

## Plan-mode draft reviews (BLTN-02)

One review per flow, following the loop in references/built-ins.md (finish the draft, `EnterPlanMode()`, the draft and an "On approval" list in the plan, `ExitPlanMode()`; "keep planning" feedback becomes `## Requested changes`). The ratchet checks that each span presents a "draft", has an `ExitPlanMode()` and sits under a skip rule.

- **plan-objective (TRD 62-05).** New step 13.5, Review TRD Drafts, after the checker passes (or after the planner when the checker is off) and before step 14. The plan holds the wave table, each TRD's goal, tasks, files and requirements, the checker verdict and the estimate; on approval the TRDs are pushed (`plan push`, which does nothing in local mode, where the planner has already committed them). Requested changes are applied by re-running the planner in revision mode, then the checker, then the review again. Step 5 becomes a printed planning-strategy block with no plan mode and no approval, so the flow has one approval. The planner is told not to push; step 10 pushes at once only when 13.5 is skipped. Skip when `--auto`, `--gaps` or `workflow.auto_advance` is true.
- **new-project (TRD 62-06).** Three reviews. PROJECT.md (step 4): the full text in the plan, reviewed before its `doc put`. REQUIREMENTS.md (step 7): the full list in the plan, replacing `Does this capture what you're building? (yes / adjust)`. ROADMAP.md (step 8): the proposed-roadmap table and per-objective details in the plan, replacing the Approve / Adjust objectives / Review full file question; Requested changes re-spawn the roadmapper as today. Skip on `--auto` only: new-project writes `workflow.auto_advance: true` into every new config, so keying the skip on it would remove the roadmap approval for every interactive user.
- **milestone complete (TRD 62-07).** One review of the MILESTONES entry draft and the PROJECT.md evolution draft before either is published: a new step `review_drafts` right after `evolve_project_full_review`, and the `doc put PROJECT.md` moves into it after approval. The MILESTONES entry is still recorded by `milestone put` in `archive_milestone`. Skip when `--auto` or `workflow.auto_advance` is true.

Unchanged: build step 3 keeps its single strategy approval in plan mode (it presents a strategy, not a draft, and build is not a BLTN-02 flow). help.md's plan-mode paragraph describes all of this and is reworded by TRD 62-10 (see the help.md rows).
