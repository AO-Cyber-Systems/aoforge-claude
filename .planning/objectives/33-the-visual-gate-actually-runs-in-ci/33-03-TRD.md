# TRD 33-03: What the gate does when it runs and finds nothing

<objective>
33-02 made the gate reachable. That converts "always SKIPPED" into "runs, and now what?" for
every objective without a manifest — which is most of them. This TRD defines what each
outcome means, implements the policy as a pure function, rewrites Step 8c to route by it, and
pins the prose with grep assertions in the shape the repo already uses for `planner.md`.

The failure mode being designed against is explicit: replacing a gate that never runs with a
gate that always gaps is worse, because people disable it. `absent` is therefore MISSING —
loud enough that nobody thinks the surface was checked, quiet enough that the existing
backlog does not turn red overnight — with a ratchet that makes it a gap for objectives
planned after the gate became auto-required.
